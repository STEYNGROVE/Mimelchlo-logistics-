## Infrastructure decision

| Service | Verified published allowance and operating conditions | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloudflare Pages, Workers Free and D1 | Pages Functions share the Workers quota. Workers Free documents 100,000 daily requests. D1 Free documents five million rows read and 100,000 written per day, 500 MB per database, 5 GB account storage and seven-day Time Travel. Free D1 limits fail closed when exhausted. Paid limits and storage charges differ. [Pages limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits/) · [Workers pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/) · [D1 limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/) | Reuse Pages Functions and separate existing preview/production D1 bindings. A second public Worker adds no required capability here. Billing permissions were unavailable for an account-specific subscription inspection. |
| Supabase Free | Published no-charge plan includes two active projects, 500 MB of database capacity, 1 GB of file storage, 5 GB egress plus 5 GB cached egress, and 50,000 monthly active users. Free inactivity can pause a project after a week. Pro starts at US$25/month. Production Auth email uses custom SMTP. [Pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing) · [Custom SMTP](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-smtp) | The account lists active projects for unrelated products. Use a dedicated approved project and sender. No unrelated project was disabled and no upgrade was made. |
| Hatchable personal | Existing private PostgreSQL and scheduler are operational. The provider's project guide documents hourly-or-slower execution on Free, with invocations and compute counted against the account allowance. | Reuse its existing project boundary for scheduled imports and operations. No extra plan subscribed. |
| GitHub Actions | Standard GitHub-hosted runners are free on public repositories. Private compute and artifact usage depend on the plan. [Runner policy](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/how-tos/write-workflows/choose-where-workflows-run/choose-the-runner-for-a-job) | Use for public build and browser evidence. Keep freight and back-office records out of CI artifacts. |
| GroqCloud | The published free table lists `openai/gpt-oss-20b` at 30 requests/minute, 1,000/day, 8,000 tokens/minute and 200,000/day. Limits apply to the organization. The Services Agreement permits application integration subject to its terms and model conditions. [Limits](https://console.groq.com/docs/rate-limits) · [Agreement](https://console.groq.com/docs/legal/services-agreement) | Obtain an approved dedicated Free key. The app independently caps uncached calls at 24 per UTC day and six per client. The provider remains disabled. |
| HeiGIT Standard | The provider issues a free Standard key. The current endpoint uses `api.heigit.org`. The provider announced retirement of the legacy routing host. The routing and geocoding entitlements and applicable commercial terms must be checked for the owner's key. [Current API](https://api.heigit.org/) · [Plan](https://account.heigit.org/info/plans) · [Terms](https://account.heigit.org/info/tos) | The app independently caps at 120 upstream requests/day and 15/client/day. It does not persist addresses or geometry. Verify Standard authorization or approved alternative before switching on. |
| Tesseract OCR and GPS | Local Tesseract can run without a subscription, subject to its software license and an approved private runtime. Paid GPS/API plans depend on hardware and providers. [Tesseract documentation](https://tesseract-ocr.github.io/) | No document was sent to an OCR service. No GPS hardware, SDK or subscription was activated. |
